# Beautiful HTML Templates

Read `AGENTS.md` completely before creating or modifying a deck. Follow its official workflow for:

- selecting a template through `index.json`;
- copying the selected template and its sibling assets;
- adapting content without weakening the template's visual system;
- validating viewport safety and presentation behavior.

Treat `templates/` as reusable source assets. Work on a copied template in the user's project; do not edit the installed library in place.
